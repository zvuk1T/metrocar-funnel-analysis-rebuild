## Metrocar-Specific Analytical Reasoning

Distinguish the Customer Funnel from the Ride Funnel.

A customer can request multiple rides, so user-level and ride-level counts cannot be treated as interchangeable. Establish the relevant grain and keys before interpreting funnel stages, transitions or percentages.

Preserve verified SQL/pandas comparisons and their stated scope. Additional interpretations must remain distinguishable from the underlying source analysis.

The school work and later rebuild must remain historically distinguishable. A newly completed analytical extension must not be presented as original coursework.
